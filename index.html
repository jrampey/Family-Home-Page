Day 262 of 365

Haggai 1–2

Read on Bible.com Open on YouTube

Kid-friendly summary

Putting God first

The people of Israel had come home after a very hard time away. They worked on making their own houses comfortable, but God’s temple was still broken down. Through the prophet Haggai, God reminded them that He should not be pushed to the side while they focused only on themselves.

The people listened. Their leader Zerubbabel, the priest Joshua, and the rest of the people began rebuilding the temple. God encouraged them not to worry that the new temple did not look as impressive as the old one. What mattered most was that God was with them. He promised that His plans were bigger than what they could see.

God also taught them that obedience matters. Their choices had consequences, but when they turned back to Him, He encouraged them with a wonderful promise: “From this day on I will bless you.”

For Ava: We can put God first even when other things feel more exciting or important. We do not have to make everything perfect—our job is to listen, obey, and trust that God is with us.
